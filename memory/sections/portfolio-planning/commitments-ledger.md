# portfolio-planning commitments ledger

*The T12 backing store. Anything promised in an artifact becomes a row here the moment that artifact is declared sent, and one promise is one row. A spoken promise becomes a row on the owner's confirmation. Remediation dates and regulator commitments never come here and live in the `risk-regulatory` issues ledger (T12 ledger). Every review cycle opens from this table rather than from recollection of meetings. State is computed from evidence, never recalled, and the values are open, kept, missed, moved. A missed row carries its cause and its correction. A moved row carries the new due date and who agreed to move it.*
