# SaaS estate register
*The single source for what the SaaS estate is, written by parvis-itam and read by parvis-finops and parvis-vendor-eval. One row per application. Update on change, age at maintenance.*
*Utilization state is `measured`, `asserted` or `unknown`, and a measured row names how the reading was taken. Data exit is `tested`, `asserted` or `unchecked`. The renewal pointer cites the vendor-management revisit trigger that holds the dates, and no date is copied here. No seat counts, no currency and no contract terms. Definitions in parvis-memory `references/section-templates.md`.*
