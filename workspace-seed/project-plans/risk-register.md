# Program risk register
*The single source for program risks (T11 record). Reviews read this file, and risks are never re-typed into an MBR. Update on change, age at maintenance.*
*Status is one of `open`, `mitigating`, `accepted` or `closed-<date>`. Accepted means knowingly taken, so record in Mitigation who accepted it and when. Rows unreviewed for more than 90 days are flagged at the pulse. A material change is ledger-worthy, so offer the capture. Definitions in parvis-memory `references/section-templates.md`.*
